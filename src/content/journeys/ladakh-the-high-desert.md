Ladakh doesn't look like the rest of India. It's a high, cold desert on the far side of the Himalaya, closer to Tibet in culture and landscape, with monasteries on the crags above a thin green strip of fields along the Indus. Guests who have travelled all over India tell us it's the place that surprised them most.

The whole plan is built around the altitude. You fly into Leh at 3,500 metres and spend two full days doing very little: the first resting, the second walking slowly round town. Only on the fourth day do we take you anywhere higher, and only on the fifth do we cross the Khardung La into the Nubra Valley. That's slower than most Ladakh itineraries you'll find online, and it's why our guests enjoy Pangong instead of lying in a tent beside it with a headache.

The second half is a road trip: the dunes and camels of Nubra, the Balti village of Turtuk a few kilometres from the Line of Control, the river road to Pangong, a night by the lake and a 5,360-metre pass on the way home. It adds up to three long days in the car, and the road is half the reason to come.

## Who this journey suits

Travellers who have seen the classic sights of India and want something that feels like an expedition but comes with a proper bed most nights. Photographers. Anyone drawn to Buddhist culture and mountain landscape. Reasonably fit adults of any age who are honest with themselves about their health. We've had guests in their seventies sail through and guests in their thirties struggle, and the ones who struggled were the ones in a hurry. Not for children under ten, and not for anyone who needs a hot bath every night.

## How we adjust it

Add three days and we go west instead of straight back to Leh: Alchi's thousand-year-old murals, the eroded hills at Lamayuru and a homestay in the Sham valley, which is lower and gentler. If a cold camp doesn't appeal, swap the Pangong night for a second night in Nubra. A Delhi night at the end means the long flight home doesn't follow straight on from the early flight out of Leh. And if you'd like to walk, we add two or three days of village-to-village trekking at the end, once your body has adapted.
