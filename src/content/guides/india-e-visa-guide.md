Nearly every guest we host comes in on an e-Visa, and it's behind most of the questions we get before a trip. We've walked hundreds of travellers through the form. The rules change, so we check this page against the official site every quarter, and the date at the top shows when we last did.

## Who can apply

Nationals of 172 countries can apply online, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, New Zealand and every country in the European Union. You'll need an ordinary passport with at least six months left from your arrival date and two blank pages. Holders of diplomatic or official passports, and people of Pakistani origin, apply through an embassy instead.

If you hold an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) card, you don't need a visa at all.

## Which e-Visa to choose

For a single trip of up to a month, take the 30-day e-Tourist visa. If there's any chance you'll come back within the year, the one-year visa is better value and saves you a second application. All three tourist visas allow the same things in India. They differ in how long they last and how often you can enter.

| Visa | Validity | Entries | Stay per visit | Fee (most nationalities) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30-day e-Tourist | 30 days from arrival | Two | Within the 30 days | USD 10 for April to June arrivals, USD 25 for July to March |
| 1-year e-Tourist | 365 days from issue | Multiple | 90 days, or 180 days for US, UK, Canadian and Japanese citizens | About USD 40 |
| 5-year e-Tourist | 5 years from issue | Multiple | 90 days, or 180 days for US, UK, Canadian and Japanese citizens | About USD 80 |

A bank charge of 2.5% is added to every fee.

There are also e-Business, e-Medical and e-Conference visas, and an AYUSH visa for Ayurvedic treatment programmes. If your journey centres on an Ayurveda retreat of more than a few days, ask your specialist whether the AYUSH visa applies.

## How to apply

You fill in one form on the official government site, upload a passport scan and a photo, and pay by card. Step by step:

1. Use only **indianvisaonline.gov.in**. Dozens of look-alike sites charge three to ten times the fee to fill in the same form for you. The official site carries no agency branding.
2. Have these ready: a scan of your passport's photo page, a recent passport-style photo on a plain background (JPEG), your flight details, your hotel for the first night (we send you a confirmation letter for this) and a credit or debit card.
3. Fill in the form slowly. Most rejections come from a name that doesn't exactly match the passport, a photo with a patterned background, or a passport that expires too soon.
4. Pay the fee. An application ID arrives by email. Keep it.
5. Approval usually comes by email within 72 hours as an Electronic Travel Authorisation. Print it. The airline checks it at the departure gate, and immigration stamps it when you land.

## Timing

Apply no later than four days before your flight. The earliest date has changed: the official portal used to accept the 30-day visa only within 30 days of arrival, and since December 2025 it has been reported to accept arrival dates up to 120 days ahead. The date picker on indianvisaonline.gov.in shows the current limit. We suggest applying two to four weeks ahead. That's early enough to fix a mistake, and late enough that a changed flight won't matter.

## On arrival

Join the queue marked e-Visa. Have your printed authorisation, your passport and your first-night hotel address in your hand. Immigration officers take your fingerprints and a photograph. At Delhi airport the whole thing usually takes 20 to 45 minutes, depending on how many flights have just landed.

## Mistakes we see every season

- **Applying through a third-party site** and paying USD 100 or more for a USD 25 visa.
- **A photo that fails.** Selfies, sunglasses, patterned backgrounds and group photos are all rejected.
- **A passport with under six months left.** Renew it first.
- **Assuming the e-Visa covers Protected Areas.** Parts of Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Ladakh's border zones and some island groups need an extra permit. We arrange it when your journey goes there.
- **Flying into a non-designated airport.** It's rare, but check if you're arriving on a small regional flight from a neighbouring country.
