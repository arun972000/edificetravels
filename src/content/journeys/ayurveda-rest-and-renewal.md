Ayurveda is India's own system of medicine, several thousand years old, and Kerala is where it's practised most seriously. A proper programme starts with an hour in front of a physician. After that come two treatments a day for as long as you can give it, a diet set for your constitution, yoga at dawn and bed by half past nine. People arrive tired, over-caffeinated and sleeping badly, and most of them leave a different shape.

We've designed this journey to protect the programme. One night in Kochi to land, then ten nights at a residential retreat in the foothills of Palakkad, where there's nothing to do but the treatments, the food, the yoga and the birds. Then two nights by the sea at Marari, because the doctors ask that you re-enter the world gently, and a long-haul flight straight off the treatment table is the wrong way to do it.

The retreats we use are hospitals first. The physicians are qualified in Ayurvedic medicine, the pharmacies make their own oils, and the kitchens cook to a prescription. Plenty of places on the Kerala coast offer a massage and call it Ayurveda, and the difference matters.

## Who this journey suits

Anyone who is simply exhausted. Travellers with a specific complaint, such as back or joint pain, stress, insomnia or digestive trouble, who want to give a different approach a proper try. Solo travellers, for whom a retreat is a safe and sociable way to spend two weeks in India. Couples who want to do something restorative together, though the programme is individual and you'll sleep a great deal. Not for anyone who wants to see India while they're at it. This is a journey to one place.

## How we adjust it

Extend to 14 or 21 nights at the retreat and we move the beach days to match; the strictest centres require 14, and we'll tell you which. If you'd rather be near the coast than in the hills, we move you to Kalari Rasayana, on a lake near Kollam, or to a centre on the Thrissur coast. Add a week of Kerala travel beforehand if you want to see the backwaters and tea hills before you switch off, and we put the retreat last. Guests who care most about yoga can swap in a retreat with a stronger yoga programme.
