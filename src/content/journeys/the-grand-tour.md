If you have three weeks and only one trip to India in you, this is the journey we'd suggest. It joins the two parts of the country people most want to see, the Mughal and Rajput north and the Kerala coast, with one flight between them and no doubling back.

The first ten days follow the classic route west, Delhi to Udaipur, with the land drying out as you go. You see the Taj twice, ride up to Amber by jeep before the buses, walk Jodhpur's blue lanes with a historian and spend a sunset in a rowing boat on Lake Pichola. Then a free day and a flight south. Kerala starts with Fort Kochi's churches and fishing nets. From there you climb to the tea estates of Munnar, stop at Periyar for the elephants and spend a night on a houseboat, before three nights on the beach at Kovalam. Most people go home rested, which is rare after three weeks of travel.

Three weeks sounds like a long time to be on the move. It isn't, because the second half hardly moves at all. The longest drive in Kerala is four hours, and from day seventeen your biggest decision is whether to swim before or after breakfast.

## Who this journey suits

First-time visitors with the time to see India properly. Families with children from about eight who want forts and camels and then a beach; this is our most-booked long route for them. Couples marking an anniversary or a retirement. Anyone who has been offered a sabbatical. It isn't for travellers who want a different bed every night, since we've built in two- and three-night stays throughout.

## How we adjust it

Add three days and we put Ranthambore's tigers between Agra and Jaipur. Swap Kovalam for Marari if you want a quieter beach a short drive from Kochi, which also cuts out the last flight. If dunes matter more to you than a blue city, trade the Jodhpur nights for the desert at Jaisalmer. Wellness-minded guests can spend the last four nights at an Ayurveda retreat by the sea, with a doctor's consultation and daily treatments. A full course needs at least seven nights, and our Ayurveda journey is built around one. Families with teenagers often give Udaipur's free day to Jodhpur instead, for the zip line over Mehrangarh's walls.
