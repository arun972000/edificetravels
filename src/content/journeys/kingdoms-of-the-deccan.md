Most visitors to the south go to Kerala and Tamil Nadu. Karnataka, just north of both, is the state they miss, and it has the ruin we'd put first in southern India. Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara empire. Its temples, palaces and kilometre-long bazaars lie scattered across a valley of granite boulders beside a green river. In 1500 it had half a million people, when Rome had fewer than a hundred thousand. It was sacked in 1565 and left as it fell.

This journey builds up to it. A night in Bengaluru to land, then Mysore, the maharaja's city with its palace and flower market. Two nights on a coffee estate in the hills of Coorg, where you'll want a blanket at night. Then one long day on the road to Hampi and three nights there, which is the right number: one for the sacred centre, one for the royal centre, one to climb a hill at dawn and do nothing much. A night among the cave temples of Badami, where this architecture began, and then down through the ghats to Goa for three nights of churches, Portuguese houses and the sea.

The pace is uneven on purpose. The middle of the trip asks a lot of you, with early starts and a long drive. The two ends ask almost nothing.

## Who this journey suits

Travellers who have done the Golden Triangle and Kerala and want somewhere less visited. Anyone with an interest in history or architecture, since Hampi and the Chalukya sites are among the great ruins of the world and almost empty. Coffee people. Couples who want culture and then a beach, in that order. It's less suited to young children, because of the long drive and the walking at Hampi, and to anyone who needs a five-star hotel every night. Badami doesn't have one.

## How we adjust it

A night at Hassan breaks the long drive and adds the Hoysala temples of Belur and Halebidu, the finest carving in the south. If you'd rather see elephants than coffee, swap Coorg for a wildlife lodge on the Kabini river. History readers can add Bijapur's Gol Gumbaz between Badami and Goa for the Deccan sultanates. And if ruins beat beaches for you, cut Goa to two nights and give the third to Hampi.
