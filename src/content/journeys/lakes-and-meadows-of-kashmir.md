Kashmir is where Mughal emperors went every summer to escape the heat of the plains, and it still works that way. Delhi in May is past forty degrees. Two hours north by air, Srinagar is cool and green, the lake is still, and the gardens the emperors built are full of families having picnics under the chinar trees.

The ten days start with the Taj, because most people flying into Delhi want to see it and the timing works. Then a morning flight north to Srinagar and two nights on a houseboat, the carved cedar boats the British built when they weren't allowed to own land in the valley. After that we take you up to the meadow at Gulmarg for a night, across the valley to Pahalgam for two, and back to Srinagar for a last night on the shore and a Kashmiri feast.

It's an unhurried route. The longest drive is four and a half hours, broken with stops, and the walks are as short or as long as you like. If you get up early once, make it for the dawn on the lake, with the vegetable sellers paddling past.

## Who this journey suits

Couples, families and older travellers who want cool air, green country and an easy pace. Indian families have holidayed here for generations, and it's a good journey for families from the diaspora who want to show friends the valley their parents talked about. It's less suited to anyone who wants long treks or big monuments every day; for those, we'd add Ladakh or Rajasthan.

## Please read this before you book

Several governments advise against travel to Jammu and Kashmir, outside Ladakh, and travel insurance may not cover you there. We say this plainly because it matters. Read your own government's advice and check your insurance before you commit. We check the local situation before confirming and again before you fly, and if things change we'll re-route you.

## How we adjust it

Skip Agra if you have seen the Taj before, and give the night to a second evening on the houseboat. Add a night at Sonamarg, on the road towards Ladakh, for its glacier walks. In winter we turn it round: Gulmarg for snow and skiing, with the houseboat heated and the lake sometimes frozen at the edges. And with another week, we drive you over the Zoji La to Ladakh.
